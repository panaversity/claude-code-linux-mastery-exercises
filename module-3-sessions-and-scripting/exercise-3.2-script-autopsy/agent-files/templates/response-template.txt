Customer Support Response Template
====================================

Greeting:
  Hello {customer_name}, thank you for reaching out to our support team.

Acknowledgment:
  I understand you're asking about: {query_summary}

Response:
  {generated_response}

Follow-up:
  Is there anything else I can help you with today?

Escalation (if confidence < threshold):
  I want to make sure you get the best possible help. Let me connect you
  with a specialist who can assist you further. Your ticket number is
  #{ticket_id}.

Closing:
  Thank you for your patience. Have a great day!

---
Variables:
  {customer_name}     - From session user profile
  {query_summary}     - First 100 chars of query
  {generated_response} - Model output
  {ticket_id}         - Auto-generated on escalation
